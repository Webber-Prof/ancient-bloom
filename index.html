Method

Four weeks from field to jar. Mostly waiting.

Nothing here is complicated. It's just slow, and slow is the part most brands skip.

01 — Sourced

Bought small

Certified organic where certification exists, farm-direct or wild-harvested where it doesn't. Saffron from Pampore, rose from Kannauj, coconut oil pressed within the month.

02 — Infused

Six weeks, no heat

Botanicals sit in oil in glass, away from light, and are turned by hand every few days. Heat would do it in an afternoon and take the good part with it.

03 — Poured

Forty at a time

Filtered twice, poured into sterilised amber glass, capped and weighed. Around forty jars a batch — the most two people can do in a day without rushing.

04 — Rested

Fourteen days

Every batch sits a fortnight before it's sold, so the scent settles and anything that was going to separate has. Then it's labelled with the date it was poured.

The label, in plain words

What goes in, and what never does.

Full ingredient lists are printed on every jar and posted on Instagram with each batch. Nothing is hidden behind "fragrance" or "proprietary blend".

In

  • Cold-pressed carrier oils, bought in small quantities and used within the season
  • Whole botanicals — dried, not extracted
  • Unrefined butters: kokum, mango, shea
  • Essential oils and absolutes, at low percentages
  • Beeswax from a single apiary in the Nilgiris
  • Amber glass and aluminium — both refillable, both recyclable

Out

  • Synthetic fragrance and "parfum"
  • Parabens, phthalates, sulphates
  • Mineral oil, silicones, petrolatum
  • Synthetic colour
  • Anything tested on animals, at any stage
  • Claims we can't back up on a label
Using it

Two short routines, not ten steps.

If you buy one thing, buy the Bloom Oil. Everything else is built around it.

Morning

  1. Rinse with plain water. No cleanser — you cleaned last night.
  2. Bloom Oil — two drops, pressed in rather than rubbed.
  3. Sunscreen. We don't make one; use whichever you'll actually reapply.

Evening

  1. Ubtan No.1 — a spoonful mixed with milk, worked in for a minute.
  2. Clay Mask — twice a week only, ten minutes, rinsed before it dries hard.
  3. Bloom Oil — three drops, warmed between the palms first.
  4. Hair Oil No.3 — Sunday night, an hour before you wash it out.

Questions

The ones we're asked most.

Why does the colour change from batch to batch?

Because the plants do. Turmeric grown in a wet year is paler; rose petals dried in March are deeper than the ones dried in July. We don't correct for it with colourant, so the jar you get in October won't exactly match the one from April. The formula is identical.

How long does it keep?

Six months from the pour date printed on the base, kept out of direct sun with the lid on. The oils and balms will go past that quite happily; the ubtan and clay mask are dry, so they last longest. If anything ever smells sharp or off, stop using it and tell us — we'll replace it.

Should I patch test?

Yes, always, with any new product. A little on the inside of your forearm, left alone for a day. Natural doesn't mean non-reactive — plenty of people react to sandalwood, nut oils or citrus. Full ingredient lists are on every jar so you can check before you buy.

Is it safe during pregnancy?

Some of the botanicals we use aren't generally recommended in pregnancy, and it depends on the product. Message us with what you're looking at and we'll send you the full ingredient list — then run it past your doctor or midwife rather than us. We're not qualified to make that call for you.

Is everything certified organic?

Not all of it, and we'd rather say so. Where a certified organic supplier exists at a scale we can buy from, we use them. Where they don't — some of the wild-harvested herbs, the beeswax — we buy direct from growers we've visited. "Organic and natural" on our label means that, not a certificate we've bought.

Can I order in bulk, or for a wedding?

Yes. Favours, hampers and corporate gifting are a good chunk of what we do. Give us four weeks' notice — a batch takes that long — and tell us the quantity and budget. We can do custom labels and unscented versions.

Do you take returns?

On unopened jars, within seven days, yes — you pay return postage. Once a jar's been opened we can't resell it, so we don't take it back. If something arrived broken or wrong, send us a photo and we'll fix it, no argument.
